Student Housing & Roommate Finder[1][2]
Requirement Analysis
Functional Requirements
1. Landlords can post property listings with rich media, including photos, video tours, floor plans, and 360° walkthroughs.
2. Users can filter housing listings by rent range, distance from campus, furnished/unfurnished status, utilities included, gender preference, and lease duration.
3. Users can browse listings on an interactive map view showing distance to campus gates, bus stops, and nearby amenities.
4. Users can bookmark properties to a saved listings watchlist and receive notifications when rent drops or availability changes.
5. Landlords can mark move-in dates on an availability calendar, and students can filter listings by their required move-in timeframe.
6. Students can complete a lifestyle compatibility quiz covering sleep schedule, cleanliness, noise tolerance, guests, smoking, and study habits to generate a compatibility score with potential roommates.
7. Students can build a public roommate profile listing their budget, preferred area, course, and personality tags.
8. Students can browse a smart match feed of compatible roommates sorted by match percentage rather than location alone.
9. Students can send and accept roommate requests before committing to a property together.
10. Groups of 2-4 students can form a roommate group and search for a flat together as a single unit.
11. Landlords can upload ownership documents and receive a verification badge once approved by an admin.
12. Landlords can manage all their listings, tenant contacts, and vacancies from a single multi-property dashboard[3]. (will have graph,bar charts)
13. Students and landlords can communicate directly through in-app messaging without sharing personal phone numbers.
14. Students can flag scam listings, misleading photos, or unsafe conditions for admin review[4] and will have automated fraud detection.
15. The system calculates each roommate's fair share of rent based on room size and amenities.
16. Roommates can log shared bills, such as electricity, internet, and groceries, and track who owes what.
17. Students can set their rent due date and receive recurring payment reminders before it hits.
18. Landlords can upload digital lease agreements, and students can read, annotate, and digitally sign them within the platform.
19. Students and landlords can jointly complete a move-in condition report with photos to avoid deposit disputes.
20. The system displays a neighborhood insights dashboard with average rent trends, safety ratings, nearby amenities, and student population density for each listing area.

Modules
Module 1: Housing Discovery
1. Landlords can post property listings with rich media, including photos, video tours, floor plans, and 360° walkthroughs.
2. Users can filter housing listings by rent range, distance from campus, furnished/unfurnished status, utilities included, gender preference, and lease duration.
3. Users can browse listings on an interactive map view showing distance to campus gates, bus stops, and nearby amenities.
4. Users can bookmark properties to a saved listings watchlist and receive notifications when rent drops or availability changes.
5. Landlords can mark move-in dates on an availability calendar, and students can filter listings by their required move-in timeframe.
Module 2: Roommate Matching
6. Students can complete a lifestyle compatibility quiz covering sleep schedule, cleanliness, noise tolerance, guests, smoking, and study habits to generate a compatibility score with potential roommates.
7. Students can build a public roommate profile listing their budget, preferred area, course, and personality tags.
8. Students can browse a smart match feed of compatible roommates sorted by match percentage rather than location alone.
9. Students can send and accept roommate requests before committing to a property together.
10. Groups of 2-4 students can form a roommate group and search for a flat together as a single unit.
Module 3: Landlord & Property Management
11. Landlords can upload ownership documents and receive a verification badge once approved by an admin.
12. Landlords can manage all their listings, tenant contacts, and vacancies from a single multi-property dashboard.
13. Students can request property viewings, and landlords can approve time slots through a shared scheduling calendar.
14. Landlords can review student applications and approve or reject them with notes through a tenant application manager.
Module 4: Communication & Safety
15. Students and landlords can communicate directly through in-app messaging without sharing personal phone numbers.
16. Students can verify their identity via university email to unlock a verified student badge and full platform access.
17. Students can rate landlords on responsiveness, accuracy, and fairness after their stay.
18. Students can flag scam listings, misleading photos, or unsafe conditions for admin review.
Module 5: Financial Tools, Legal Documentation & Insights
19. The system calculates each roommate's fair share of rent based on room size and amenities.
20. Roommates can log shared bills, such as electricity, internet, and groceries, and track who owes what.
21. Students can set their rent due date and receive recurring payment reminders before it hits.
22. Users can record the security deposit paid, terms, and expected return date, with landlord acknowledgment.
23. Landlords can upload digital lease agreements, and students can read, annotate, and digitally sign them within the platform.
24. Students and landlords can jointly complete a move-in condition report with photos to avoid deposit disputes.
25. The system displays a neighborhood insights dashboard with average rent trends, safety ratings, nearby amenities, and student population density for each listing area.
Module 6: Move-In Support Services
26. Students can subscribe to local catering or mess meal plans for daily meals when they do not yet have kitchen access in their new place.
27. Students can request utility connection assistance for gas, electricity, water, and WiFi setup when moving into a new rental.
28. Students can book on-call repair and maintenance services, such as electricians, plumbers, and AC technicians, for issues in their rented accommodation.
