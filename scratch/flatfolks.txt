Student Housing & Roommate Finder
Requirement Analysis
Functional Requirements
	•	Landlords can post property listings with rich media, including photos, video tours, floor plans, and 360° walkthroughs.
	•	Users can filter housing listings by rent range, distance from campus, furnished/unfurnished status, utilities included, gender preference, and lease duration.
	•	Users can browse listings on an interactive map view showing distance to campus gates, bus stops, and nearby amenities.
	•	Users can bookmark properties to a saved listings watchlist and receive notifications when rent drops or availability changes.
	•	Landlords can mark move-in dates on an availability calendar, and students can filter listings by their required move-in timeframe.
	•	Students can complete a lifestyle compatibility quiz covering sleep schedule, cleanliness, noise tolerance, guests, smoking, and study habits to generate a compatibility score with potential roommates.
	•	Students can build a public roommate profile listing their budget, preferred area, course, and personality tags.
	•	Students can browse a smart match feed of compatible roommates sorted by match percentage rather than location alone.
	•	Students can send and accept roommate requests before committing to a property together.
	•	Groups of 2–4 students can form a roommate group and search for a flat together as a single unit.
	•	Landlords can upload ownership documents and receive a verification badge once approved by an admin.
	•	Landlords can manage all their listings, tenant contacts, and vacancies from a single multi-property dashboard. (will have graph,bar charts)
	•	Students and landlords can communicate directly through in-app messaging without sharing personal phone numbers.
	•	Students can flag scam listings, misleading photos, or unsafe conditions for admin review and will have automated fraud detection.
	•	The system calculates each roommate's fair share of rent based on room size and amenities.
	•	Roommates can log shared bills, such as electricity, internet, and groceries, and track who owes what.
	•	Students can set their rent due date and receive recurring payment reminders before it hits.
	•	Landlords can upload digital lease agreements, and students can read, annotate, and digitally sign them within the platform.
	•	Students and landlords can jointly complete a move-in condition report with photos to avoid deposit disputes.
	•	The system displays a neighborhood insights dashboard with average rent trends, safety ratings, nearby amenities, and student population density for each listing area.

Modules
Module 1: Housing Discovery
	•	Landlords can post property listings with rich media, including photos, video tours, floor plans, and 360° walkthroughs.
	•	Users can filter housing listings by rent range, distance from campus, furnished/unfurnished status, utilities included, gender preference, and lease duration.
	•	Users can browse listings on an interactive map view showing distance to campus gates, bus stops, and nearby amenities.
	•	Users can bookmark properties to a saved listings watchlist and receive notifications when rent drops or availability changes.
	•	Landlords can mark move-in dates on an availability calendar, and students can filter listings by their required move-in timeframe.
Module 2: Roommate Matching
	•	Students can complete a lifestyle compatibility quiz covering sleep schedule, cleanliness, noise tolerance, guests, smoking, and study habits to generate a compatibility score with potential roommates.
	•	Students can build a public roommate profile listing their budget, preferred area, course, and personality tags.
	•	Students can browse a smart match feed of compatible roommates sorted by match percentage rather than location alone.
	•	Students can send and accept roommate requests before committing to a property together.
	•	Groups of 2–4 students can form a roommate group and search for a flat together as a single unit.
Module 3: Landlord & Property Management
	•	Landlords can upload ownership documents and receive a verification badge once approved by an admin.
	•	Landlords can manage all their listings, tenant contacts, and vacancies from a single multi-property dashboard.
	•	Students can request property viewings, and landlords can approve time slots through a shared scheduling calendar.
	•	Landlords can review student applications and approve or reject them with notes through a tenant application manager.
Module 4: Communication & Safety
	•	Students and landlords can communicate directly through in-app messaging without sharing personal phone numbers.
	•	Students can verify their identity via university email to unlock a verified student badge and full platform access.
	•	Students can rate landlords on responsiveness, accuracy, and fairness after their stay.
	•	Students can flag scam listings, misleading photos, or unsafe conditions for admin review.
Module 5: Financial Tools, Legal Documentation & Insights
	•	The system calculates each roommate's fair share of rent based on room size and amenities.
	•	Roommates can log shared bills, such as electricity, internet, and groceries, and track who owes what.
	•	Students can set their rent due date and receive recurring payment reminders before it hits.
	•	Users can record the security deposit paid, terms, and expected return date, with landlord acknowledgment.
	•	Landlords can upload digital lease agreements, and students can read, annotate, and digitally sign them within the platform.
	•	Students and landlords can jointly complete a move-in condition report with photos to avoid deposit disputes.
	•	The system displays a neighborhood insights dashboard with average rent trends, safety ratings, nearby amenities, and student population density for each listing area.
Module 6: Move-In Support Services
	•	Students can subscribe to local catering or mess meal plans for daily meals when they do not yet have kitchen access in their new place.
	•	Students can request utility connection assistance for gas, electricity, water, and WiFi setup when moving into a new rental.
	•	Students can book on-call repair and maintenance services, such as electricians, plumbers, and AC technicians, for issues in their rented accommodation.
